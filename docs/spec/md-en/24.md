# Annex D Not in this edition (informative)

(1) The following have a place in the design but are not accepted by the processor of this edition; using them is refused at load. This document does not define their meaning until a later edition does.

- Floating point: the type `float` , float literals (6.4 (8); they take an exponent, since a number with a point alone is a `dec` , 7.5), float slots, the module `float` , `fmt.fixed` , record fields `f32` and `f64` .
- Declarations defined by users ( `make` , 6.6 (5)).
- Records held inside records, and the field-by-field layout `columns` .
- The compression module `deflate` .
